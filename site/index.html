Compatibility

Will it run on my phone?

It depends on your kernel, not the app.

DeviceMethodNotes
GKI (kernel 5.10+)LKM / boot patchRecommended. Use the .ko that matches your KMI.
Non-GKI (4.14+)Custom kernelIntegrate DarkNightSu into your kernel source and build it.
KPMCONFIG_KPM=y64-bit kernels. Non-GKI also needs CONFIG_KALLSYMS_ALL=y.
⚠ Read this. If your phone's kernel does not support kernel root, stay away. DarkNightSu can't force root onto an unsupported kernel, and a wrong flash can brick your device. Always back up your original boot image.
Install

Three steps.

Get a compatible kernel

Flash a supported kernel, or load the LKM or patch your boot.img from the manager.

Install the APK

Only the official -release.apk from Releases. Debug and CI builds are rejected by the kernel.

Open and personalise

Pick your palette, UI style and dock. Done.
